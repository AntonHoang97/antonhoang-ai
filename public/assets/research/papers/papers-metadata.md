```yaml
github: ""
featured: true
```

**Short description:**  
This retrospective diagnostic accuracy study evaluated contrast enema for suspected Hirschsprung disease in 354 children during COVID-19-related disruption, when preoperative rectal suction biopsy was unavailable.

**Website card text:**  
A retrospective diagnostic-accuracy study assessing contrast enema as a pragmatic triage tool for suspected Hirschsprung disease during COVID-19 service disruption in Vietnam.

**Key result for website:**  
Contrast enema showed moderate to high diagnostic accuracy, with sensitivity of 86.75%, specificity of 90.64%, positive predictive value of 87.33%, and negative predictive value of 90.20%. The study emphasizes that contrast enema should not replace histological confirmation when tissue diagnosis is available.

**Tags:**  
`paediatric surgery`, `Hirschsprung disease`, `contrast enema`, `diagnostic accuracy`, `COVID-19`, `Vietnam`, `radiology`
