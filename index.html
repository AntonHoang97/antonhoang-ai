Profile

A clinician building research, data and medical technology systems.

Trained as a medical doctor at Hanoi Medical University, I run operations, quality and data systems for personalized 3D-printed medical devices at VinUniversity's Institute of 3D Technology in Medicine, working with surgeons at 18 hospitals.

Around that work I build the software and the evidence: a case management system covering more than 1,300 clinical cases, a quality system certified to ISO 13485, national standards for medical 3D printing, and the reproducible analysis behind peer-reviewed publications.

01

VCMS

Case management system / in use since 2022

VCMS runs the medical 3D printing service of VinUniversity's Institute of 3D Technology in Medicine. I designed and built it end to end: one traceable record for every clinical case, from the surgeon's request through imaging, design, polymer and metal printing, QA/QC and delivery, with inventory, costing, contracts and an executive dashboard on top. 02

3D Technology in Medicine

Quality, regulatory, clinical engineering / VinUniversity, Vinmec

As operations and ISO lead I built the quality management system behind the service, certified to ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 13485:2016, with design controls, risk management, traceability and CAPA. I took the personalized surgical guide to a Class B medical device declaration, drafted in-house standards for five product lines, and coordinate the drafting of national standards (TCVN) for medical 3D printing with the Ministry of Health. The clinical side covers surgical planning with surgeons, patient-specific guides, anatomical models and customized titanium implants. 03

Lower Mekong Water Quality

Data analysis, interactive site / R, Shiny, JavaScript

Ten years of Mekong River Commission monitoring, 2011 to 2020: 42,858 observations from 24 stations in four countries, across 18 indicators. I am co-first author of the article in Total Environment Advances (2026) and did its software, data curation and visualisation. The analysis covers Mann-Kendall trends with Sen's slope, seasonal clustering and principal component analysis. I then built the companion site, where every figure of the article is recomputed in R from the monitoring record and the build stops if a number differs from the printed one.
