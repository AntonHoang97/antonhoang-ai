## 7. About page

### Page purpose

Tell my professional story in a clear and human way.

### Draft narrative

I was trained as a medical doctor at Hanoi Medical University and later developed my work at the intersection of clinical medicine, data systems, and healthcare technology implementation.

At VinUniversity and Vinmec, I have worked on clinical-engineering workflows for personalized 3D medical technology, including clinical request intake, CT/MRI/CBCT imaging data, 3D segmentation, patient-specific design, production coordination, QA/QC documentation, traceability, and clinical deployment.

Over time, my work has expanded into healthcare data science, reproducible analysis pipelines, dashboard development, institutional research intelligence, and biomedical data analysis. I am particularly interested in how AI-ready and regulatory-grade data systems can support safer, more scalable, and more intelligent medical technology development.

This website brings together my research, applied work, publications, analysis workflows, and future teaching resources.

The website should feel like:
- Personal research hub
- Academic but modern
- Healthcare deeptech
- Editorial portfolio
- Structured and expandable

It should not feel like:
- A generic CV page
- A SaaS landing page
- A designer portfolio
- A hospital website
- A random blog

- Premium editorial spacing inspired by laugon.com
- Academic credibility
- Healthcare technology seriousness

- Strong typography
- Subtle motion only

- Off-white
- Muted red / mahogany
- Warm gold
- Neutral gray
